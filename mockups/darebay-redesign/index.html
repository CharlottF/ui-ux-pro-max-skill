Differences

Why this is not the same as ordering clips

Views and sales in one task

On other platforms, payment for views and an affiliate program are two different products with separate payouts. Here it is one task card, one payout and one report.

We count what we pay for

Views go through a check for artificial inflation, and only an order that was paid for and not refunded counts as a sale. The word "counted" here means "checked".

A network proven by sales

Creators are ranked not by follower counts but by paid orders they have actually generated — the ones who don't sell simply don't get new tasks.
